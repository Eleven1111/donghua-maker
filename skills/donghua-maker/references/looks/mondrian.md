# Look: mondrian / 格子构成

White planes cut by thick black lines, with a few red, yellow and blue planes (and maybe one grey) in asymmetric balance. Lines draw in on beats and planes fill on notes. A "boogie" mode turns the grid into yellow lanes with small colour blocks travelling like traffic. The construction was studied from public-domain Neo-Plastic paintings. Compositions are generated by recursive splitting, never copied.

Verified in 格子里的节奏 (`assets/example-mondrian.html`, 16:9, 12 s, 3 shots, 120 bpm).

## Toolkit (`toolkit-mondrian.js`)
| call | what it does |
|---|---|
| `mdCompose(x, y, w, h, seed, {depth})` | recursive split into `rects` and `lines` (lines in split order). Colours go to the largest, a middle and a small plane. |
| `mdDraw(g, C, u, fill(r))` | draws the composition: `u` reveals the lines in order; `fill(r)` returns 0..1 per plane (fills rise from the bottom edge) |
| `mdBoogie(g, x, y, w, h, t, seed)` | yellow lane grid with moving blocks and nested squares, as a pure function of t |

## Rules
- Line width is about 26 px at 2560. Keep 3 colour planes, 4 at most. Most planes stay white.
- One line per beat and one fill per note: the grid is the rhythm.
- Seeds change the composition. Check that the largest plane isn't centred (asymmetry).
